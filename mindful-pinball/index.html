Mindful Pinball

Stay with the ball. Keep it in play as long as you can.

Touch the left side of the screen for the left flipper and the right side for the right flipper. Hold to keep a flipper up.

To launch, drag the plunger (bottom right) down and let go. Pull further for a stronger shot.

Keyboard: Z / Left Shift and / / Right Shift for flippers, Space or Down to pull the plunger.
